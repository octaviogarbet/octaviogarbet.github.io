I believe the hardest part of software engineering isn't the code—it’s human alignment, communication, and clear execution. Throughout my career, whether managing a 30-developer frontend department or leading 100% remote cross-functional squads, I prioritize psychological safety, active feedback, and personal career growth. When engineers feel supported and understand the business <em>why</em> behind their work, high performance follows naturally.

I have a track record of guiding colleagues in their careers and building a knowledge-sharing culture that develops new leaders across the organization. My leadership role has grown to managing engineers across several teams. I'm also a presenter at conferences, meetups and internal events.

I’ve also served as an Adjunct Professor at ORT University, teaching software architecture, API design, and client-server systems.
